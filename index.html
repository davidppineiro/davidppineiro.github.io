David Pérez Piñeiro

Papers Teaching Press

I am a research scientist at Gridmatic, where I build models and algorithms for quantitative trading in electricity markets.

I like finding optimization problems in real-world applications and designing reliable and efficient algorithms to solve them. Most of my work is in control, energy management, and portfolio construction.

I received a BSc in Energy Engineering from the University of Vigo and an MSc in Sustainable Energy from NTNU, supported by a “la Caixa” fellowship. I am now completing my PhD at NTNU, advised by Sigurd Skogestad, and have been a visiting researcher with Richard Braatz at MIT and Stephen Boyd at Stanford.
