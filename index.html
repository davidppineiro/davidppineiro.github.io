About me

I am a research scientist at Gridmatic, where I build optimization models and algorithms for quantitative energy trading.

I am interested in optimization and its applications to control, energy, and finance. I am completing my PhD at NTNU, advised by Sigurd Skogestad, and have been a visiting researcher with Richard Braatz at MIT and Stephen Boyd at Stanford.

I received a BSc in Energy Engineering from the University of Vigo and an MSc in Sustainable Energy from NTNU, supported by a “la Caixa” Foundation postgraduate fellowship.
